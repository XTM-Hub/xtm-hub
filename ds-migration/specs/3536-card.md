# Card → `@filigran/design-system`

## Intent

The legacy `Card*` parts (11 call sites, also covering #3533, #3538, #3539, #3544) move to the design system `Card`
compound. Every card keeps its content, its title text, its actions, its link and its place in the page. The surface,
the padding, the gutter between parts and the title type take the design system look.

## Props mapping

Rules shared by every row:

- **Imports**: `Card`, `CardHeader`, `CardTitle`, `CardSubtitle`, `CardContent`, `CardFooter` from
  `@filigran/design-system`. `Separator`, `Label`, `Avatar`, `AutoForm`, `toast` and the other legacy imports stay on
  `@filigran/ui`.
- **`CardContent` always gets `clamp={0}`**: the design system default clamps its text to 3 lines with
  `overflow: hidden`, which would cut every form, list and row these cards hold.
- **`CardTitle` gets `as="h3"`**, the legacy element (the design system applies no heading by default), and loses
  `className="heading-lg"`: the title takes the design system `title-sm` type.
- **Surface and padding go to the root**: `bg-elevation-background-layer-1` and the legacy `border*` classes go (Paper
  paints surface and border); `bg-elevation-background-layer-2` becomes `elevation={2}`. Padding classes on
  `CardContent` (`p-0`, `pb-0`, `p-4`, `p-s`) and on `Card` (`p-m`, `pt-s`) go. A card whose legacy inset was 24px
  keeps the default `padding`; a compact card (legacy 8 to 16px) takes `padding={16}`.
- **Layout classes stay**: sizing and placement on `Card` (`h-full`, `w-full`, `w-1-3`, `my-xs`) and the flex or grid
  layout on `CardContent` (`grid gap-l`, `flex flex-col gap-m h-full`, …).

## Decisions

- **`Card` with `CardContent`, not `Paper`, for the tiles with no header**: contract RULE-01 says a surface with no
  anatomy is a `Paper`, but the swap keeps each site on the counterpart of what it used; moving the five tiles to
  `Paper` is traced under To validate.
- **`clamp={0}` everywhere**: every `CardContent` here holds forms, lists or rows, never a teaser text to clamp.
- **`CardDescription` → `CardSubtitle`**: the design system has no description part; the subtitle is "the line under
  the title" (llms.txt), the same role.
- **`CapabilityDescription`'s content leaves the header**: `CardHeader` children form the title column; the list is the
  card's body, a sibling part like on every other site.
- **One padding rule**: 24px insets keep the default, compact tiles (legacy 8 to 16px) take 16, the nearest Paper step
  that keeps their content readable in grids. The trial guide tiles (legacy 12px, equidistant from 8 and 16) take 8:
  at 16 their content narrows by 8px and the `Academy: OpenCTI 101-301` title wraps at its hyphen in the four-column
  grid at 1280px.
- **`ResetPassword` keeps `justify-end`** on its footer: placement of its only action, not legacy styling. `Picture`
  needs none, its two buttons already split.
- **`RegisteredPlatformCard` hover uses the design system Card's own hover couple** (`bg-elevation-hover` +
  `border-elevation-subtle`) instead of the legacy `bg-hover`: the card stays a link wrapped from outside, and a linked
  card without hover would lose a state.
- **`RequestTransferPersonalSpace` drops its red border** (epic review): it recreated the legacy look on a design
  system surface. The red `Danger zone` heading and the destructive button carry the warning.
- **`CapabilityDescription`'s sentence opens `CardContent`** (epic review): as a `CardSubtitle` it was truncated on
  one line, cutting the French text below about 1200px, and cut text is a defect. As a paragraph above the list it
  wraps; the header keeps the title only.

## To validate

- Card titles shrink from `heading-lg` to the design system `title-sm`, and parts sit 24px apart (the card gap): the
  profile picture footer gains 24px under the avatar, the capabilities list moves from 6px to 24px under the subtitle.
  Alternative: none without restyling.
- Compact tiles take `padding={16}`: the homepage platform cards grow from 8px (16px on top) to 16px all round.
  Alternative: `padding={8}`.
- The trial guide tiles go from 12 to 8px (`padding={8}`) so their titles keep one line. Alternative: `padding={16}`,
  like the other tiles, with `Academy: OpenCTI 101-301` wrapping on two lines at 1280px.
- The five header-less tiles stay `Card` + `CardContent`. Alternative: `Paper` (contract RULE-01), with the body
  directly inside.
- `ResetPassword`'s button stays right-aligned. Alternative: the `CardFooter` default, left-aligned when alone.
- Linked homepage platform cards hover with the design system card hover couple. Alternative: `<Card href>` with a
  `CardTitle` (see Deferred findings).

## Deferred findings

- `RegisteredPlatformCard` wraps the whole card in a `Link`: the design system pattern is `<Card href>` with the
  platform title as `CardTitle`, which names the link by its title rather than by the whole card text.
- The trial guide and bundle tiles hand-roll an icon or logo row and a title paragraph that `CardHeader`'s `icon` slot
  and `CardTitle` cover.
- The design system has no destructive card surface for a danger zone; to raise upstream.
- `Parameters` keeps `w-1-3` on its card, a class defined nowhere, so it has no effect.
- No unit test covers `CapabilityDescription`, `Edit`, `Picture`, `ResetPassword`, `RequestTransferPersonalSpace` or
  `Parameters`, and none checks the card titles' `h3` level, which now rests on `as="h3"` alone.
