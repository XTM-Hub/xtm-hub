# OpenCTI Product Connection


To utilize features such as one-click deployments, your OpenCTI product must be connected (available from OpenCTI 6.7.10). 

The connection process is initiated from your OpenCTI product. For more detailed information, please refer to the [OpenCTI documentation](https://docs.opencti.io/latest/administration/hub).
You can also connect your product from the XTM Hub from different places: 

- From the header in the top right corner, click on "X connected products" and then click "Connect your product +"

- From the homepage if you do not have any product connected, click on the blue button "Connect your product +"

- From libraries, when you try one click deploy and have no product connected, you are able to connect directly from there. 

To connect from Hub, you just need your product URL, XTM Hub takes care of the rest! 

## View a list of your connected OpenCTI products

You can access a list of your connected OpenCTI products on the homepage. 

To locate a specific product, look under the organization where it was initially connected. You will see a tile on the homepage on which you can click on to access the newly connected product.
![OpenCTI connected product illustration](../assets/images/opencti-product-connected.png)

You can also have access to your connected products anywhere from the header in the top right corner.
![header connected products illustration](../assets/images/connected-from-header.png)


The product's name is derived directly from the title of your OpenCTI product. You can edit it on the product's page on XTM Hub or during the connection process.
